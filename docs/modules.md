# Modules

Department OS is organized around product modules, each answering a distinct question and producing a distinct artifact. All modules run on [Department OS Core](architecture.md).

## Signal Foundry

Discovers emerging products, technologies, tools, market shifts, research, opportunities, and conversations worth investigating.

**Primary question:** What is emerging that deserves attention?

## Problem Department

Finds evidence of unmet demand through recurring problems, explicit willingness to pay, existing spending, workarounds, reviews, jobs, RFPs, feature requests, and community discussions.

Researches who experiences the problem, its severity and frequency, existing solutions, incumbent weaknesses, market gaps, feasibility, and demand confidence.

**Primary question:** What do people genuinely need, and where is the unresolved demand?

**Primary artifact:** A cited Problem Brief with an evidence ledger and demand-confidence score.

This is the module targeted by the first executable milestone — see [milestones/problem-department-mvp.md](milestones/problem-department-mvp.md).

## Prototype Department

Turns a qualified opportunity into the smallest credible test or build.

Produces hypotheses, original solution directions, requirements, constraints, prototype specifications, validation experiments, tests, benchmarks, release decisions, and postmortems.

**Primary question:** What is the smallest credible thing we can build to test this opportunity?

## Creative Practice Engine

Helps multidisciplinary creators organize, understand, connect, and present their body of work.

Works across project archives, images, research, writing, proposals, unfinished ideas, biographies, and professional materials.

Produces project indexes, recurring themes, biographies, statements, portfolio narratives, proposals, applications, and connections among past and future work.

**Primary question:** How does this collection of projects become a coherent creative practice?

## Public lab

### Mandate to Build

Mandate to Build is the public experimental lab and editorial thesis. It is not primarily a customer product module.

It investigates overpriced, inaccessible, overcomplicated, or poorly served product categories; identifies what customers are actually paying for; separates the useful mechanism from unnecessary packaging; develops ethical original alternatives; and publishes the process through YouTube, Shorts, articles, and public experiments.

Selected concepts are handed to the Prototype Department for testing and development.

### Skool community

Skool is the community and participation leg of Mandate to Build.

Supports build-with-us participation, voting, templates, workflow access, experiments, office hours, community validation, and early product releases.

## Later module

### Patent Reinvention (later)

Patent Reinvention is a bonus-bonus module planned after the complete Department OS suite is built.

It will discover expired or abandoned patents, verify status, decompose mechanisms, identify contemporary recombination opportunities, stress-test feasibility, and hand qualified concepts to the Prototype Department.

**This module must not expand the scope of the first implementation.** It is documented here for completeness and roadmap purposes only.
