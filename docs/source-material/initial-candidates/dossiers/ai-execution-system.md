# Candidate Dossier: AI Execution System

## Status

`SEED — not yet evaluated by Department OS`

## Origin

Incubated in a ChatGPT project prior to Department OS, reverse-engineering a public paid offer (Peter Mesarec's "AI Execution System," petermesarec.ai). Handed off to Department OS 2026-08-07.

## Trigger

Danny: "I'm interested in the idea of what's on this link, but I'm not sure that a $97 download is worth it... can you and I sort of reverse engineer it, think through it together." This is the clearest verbatim Personal Pull statement in the corpus.

## Purchase resistance

Source-supported, in Danny's own words: explicit reluctance to pay $97 for the download, paired with an explicit ask to reverse-engineer it instead. The chat's own supporting assessment adds that the page's claimed "1,675+ trusted reviews" could not be corroborated by the assistant's search, and that the page displayed inconsistent discount figures (34% vs. 20%) — these are the assistant's observations, not independently re-verified during this handoff.

## Desired useful part

An operating system for running technology/AI initiatives together (not AI treated as a separate silo): executive mandate, workflow inventory, an opportunity-scoring model, a pilot charter, a 30-day execution cadence, an evidence ledger per pilot, and a scale/governance gate — applied to Danny's actual organization rather than sold as a generic template.

## Existing exploration

Design-stage only, per this source. The chat proposes:
- A 7-factor opportunity scorecard (business impact, frequency/scale, measurability, process/data readiness, owner/adoption readiness, implementation effort, risk/reversibility), each on a 1–5 scale.
- A specific first pilot recommendation: an "AI-assisted Technology Executive Brief" — a weekly one-page status view — chosen deliberately over flashier options.
- A companion research export (`ai-execution-system-research-export.md`) citing Microsoft Cloud Adoption Framework, NIST AI RMF, Google Cloud AI Adoption Framework, and OECD.AI guidance as structural analogues to the paid product's method.

## Emerging differentiation

The chat frames the differentiator as: build this against Danny's actual workflows and organization rather than as a generic packaged framework, and treat "AI initiatives" and ordinary technology initiatives as one portfolio rather than two. No product-level differentiation beyond that is proposed in this source — this stayed at the personal/organizational-tool level, not a market-facing product concept.
