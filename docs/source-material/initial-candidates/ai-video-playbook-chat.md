## Assistant

Oh, this is fun. I would avoid centering the name on AI, cloning, or YouTube—the studio could grow far beyond those.

### Strongest overall candidates

1. **Not Buying It**  
Perfect double meaning: skepticism toward sales claims, plus literally choosing not to buy. Memorable, funny, and naturally episodic.

“Welcome to Not Buying It. Today, we’re rebuilding a $5,000 AI clone program.”

2. **Build Instead**  
Simple, optimistic, and exactly the philosophy.

Don’t just consume it. Build instead.

3. **Worth Building**  
Every experiment asks the same question: is this useful enough to recreate—and good enough to become a product?

4. **Proof of Build**  
Sounds credible and experimental. The prototype is the evidence.

5. **Curiosity Works**  
Curiosity as both the starting point and the studio itself. Warm, intelligent, and expandable.

6. **The Rebuild Lab**  
Immediately communicates the format, although it may lean slightly more technical.

## Assistant

That’s a very strong shortlist, and I agree—not naming it too early is the right move.

I see three distinct identities emerging:

**Institutional / slightly strange:** Open Assembly, The Prototype Department  
**Industrial / builder-oriented:** First Principles Works, Signal Foundry, Mandate to Build  
**Curious / editorial:** The Useful Part, Curiosity Works

The Prototype Department has unusual personality. It sounds like a real place where experiments are assigned, documented, and released. “Department” could become a whole visual language: case files, experiment numbers, field reports, approvals, rejected prototypes.

The Useful Part may be the clearest expression of the mission.

Curiosity Works may be the broadest long-term company name.

Mandate to Build has the most attitude.

Let’s keep these alive and allow the first few experiments to reveal what the studio actually wants to be called. A good name often becomes obvious once we can see the voice, visual identity, and work together.
